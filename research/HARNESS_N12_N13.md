(session 24, `experiments/session24.py` → `results/harness_caps.json`)

**The brief:** not N6–N10 again — a *conceptual applied method* of **harnessing or
redistributing** dark energy. Two designs, both pushed to their physical limits:

## N12 — The Λ-SHIELD: a Casimir-redistribution membrane (redistribute Λ out of a volume)

**Mechanism.** A parallel-plate Casimir cavity of gap *d* carries energy density
ρ_C(d) = −π²ħc/(240 d⁴). At **d ≈ 40 μm**, |ρ_C| = ρ_Λc² = 5.3×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³ — the cavity
*locally cancels the cosmological constant*. This is redistribution, not extraction:
the vacuum energy is pushed out of the gap into the plates' stress.

**Applied machine — the vacuum-gradient piston.** Put a piston between a shielded
region (Λ_eff ≈ 0) and the ambient vacuum (Λ): the pressure differential is exactly
ρ_Λc² ≈ 5.3×10⁻¹⁰ Pa, so one collapse of a 1 m³ cavity yields **5.3×10⁻¹⁰ J**.
Resetting (pulling the plates apart) costs exactly the Casimir binding energy —
**net zero per cycle** (passivity of the ground state; the plates are the battery,
not the vacuum). The only net-positive term in any cycle is cosmic expansion
stretching the cavity: dW = ρ_Λc² dV with dV/V = 3H dt →

> **continuous ceiling = 3Hρ_Λc² = 3.4×10⁻²⁷ W/m³** (H₀ρ_Λc² = 1.1×10⁻²⁷ W/m³)

— the same cap as N6–N10. Shielding 1 m³ needs 2.5×10⁴ m² of plates at 40 μm gaps;
the plate mass-energy dwarfs the effect, and our data bound any *astrophysical*
static tilt of Λ to <0.01 mag/dex in host mass (§19). Unimodular-gravity analogy:
Λ as an integration constant can be "discharged" only globally; locally the cavity
is the only lever, and it is zero-sum.

**Verdict N12:** redistribution is *real* (Casimir cavities do cancel Λ locally —
measurable as the −5.3×10⁻¹⁰ Pa offset at gaps ≳40 μm, N8), but as a machine it is
a pump with no net output. Ceiling: one-shot 5.3×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³, continuous 3.4×10⁻²⁷ W/m³.

## N13 — The PHANTOM-DIP TURBINE (harness the growth of the vacuum)

**Mechanism.** If the z~1 dip is real (our cert5: w = **−1.27±0.16** at z∈[0.75,1.5],
§34/§36; DESI's own published w0wa chains give w(1) = −1.10…−1.36, §37), then at z~1
w < −1 and the vacuum energy density **grows with time**:

> d(ρ_Λc²)/dt = −3H(1+w)ρ_Λc² > 0 — an NEC violation: the vacuum is doing net
> positive work on the expansion. Posterior power density at z=1:
> **8×10⁻²⁸ W/m³** (68% range 5.5–10×10⁻²⁸).

**Applied machine.** A scalar field φ with a ∂_μ(ρ_Λ) coupling (an "induction coil"
for vacuum-energy current) could in principle tap this growth. Ceiling: ~10⁻²⁷ W/m³ —
26 orders of magnitude below solar flux (1361 W/m²). Dead as a power plant, but it
is the **only configuration in physics where the vacuum does net positive work**, and
DR3 + fσ8 in the dip bin decide whether it exists (pre-registered §30).

**Even with NEC violation, warp is dead:** an Alcubierre bubble at R = 100 m needs
ρ ≈ −c⁴/(8πG R²) = −4.8×10³⁸ J/m³ — that is **3.7×10⁴⁹× denser (negatively) than
the best Casimir gap** (−1.3×10⁵ J/m³ at d = 10 nm). 49 orders short.

**Verdict N13:** the phantom dip is the one place dark energy could in principle be
*tapped*; the tap's ceiling is the same 10⁻²⁷ W/m³. Probe, not resource.

## Bottom line

Every road — extraction, redistribution, thermal baths, horizon engines, warp — ends
at the same two numbers: **one-shot ≤ 5.3×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³, continuous ≤ ~10⁻²⁷ W/m³**
(~1 m³ of vacuum powers humanity for ~10⁴⁶ yr; dilution puts it 26 orders below
sunlight). Redistribution (N12) is real but zero-sum; harnessing (N13) exists only if
the z~1 dip survives DR3, and even then is capped at 10⁻²⁷ W/m³. **Dark energy is a
probe, not a resource — now demonstrated against every mechanism we could invent.**

# What the dark-energy "probe" actually probes (session 24 capstone)

If Λ is not a power source, what is it? A measurement apparatus pointed at the
vacuum and at gravity. Concretely, from our own pipeline (`RESULTS.md`):

1. **The absolute energy of the vacuum.** ρ_Λc² = 5.3×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³ is the only
   macroscopic measurement of the zero-point energy of empty space — the one number
   that says "the vacuum is not exactly zero." Against the QFT estimate (~10¹¹⁰ J/m³)
   it *is* the 10^120 problem: the largest discrepancy in physics, measured, not
   theorized. Any theory of the vacuum must reproduce 10⁻¹²⁰ of the naive sum.

2. **Whether gravity is GR + a constant.** Λ does not clump (equivalence principle
   for vacuum energy): our host-mass test put any static tilt at <0.01 mag/dex (§19),
   the N4 screened-vacuum model excluded. Growth (24-pt fσ8) tests GR's prediction
   for structure under Λ. Modified gravity must pass through the w(z) and f(z) data.

3. **Whether Λ is a constant of nature or a rolling field.** This is what w(z)
   measures. Our cert5: w(0)≈−0.91, dip w=−1.27±0.16 at z∈[0.75,1.5] (§24/§34/§36),
   corroborated by DESI's own chains w(1)=−1.1…−1.4 (§37). If the dip survives DR3,
   the vacuum is a dynamical two-field sector (quintom, N2) — a scalar field in the
   sky, i.e. new fundamental particles. If not, Λ is the simplest constant in the
   Lagrangian and the probe says "nothing new."

4. **The fate of the universe.** w decides the ending: w=−1 → eternal de Sitter
   expansion and heat death (horizon T = ħH/2πk ≈ 2.7×10⁻³⁰ K, §37); w<−1 → Big Rip;
   w>−1 → possible recollapse. The probe reads the last chapter.

5. **The coincidence problem.** Ω_Λ ~ Ω_m *today* — why now? Dynamical explanations
   (running vacuum) tested and null (ν=0.008±0.005, §22); landscape/anthropic
   remains. The probe constrains the measure problem.

6. **Calibration itself.** The 2025–26 "3σ evolving DE" turned out to be a
   +0.20±0.03 mag (6.7σ) offset in the 77 Cepheid-calibrator SNe (§27) — the
   BAO+SN+CMB combination caught a systematic in the local distance ladder. The
   probe works both ways: it reads new physics *or* it reads our own calibration.

7. **The matter-dominated era.** The 2026 DR2 Lyα full-shape AP (1% at z=2.33) is
   the cleanest anchor at z>1: ΛCDM-consistent alone (χ²=0.97/2), Lyα+BBN
   H₀=66.5±1.3 (§33) — it reads the expansion where dark energy was negligible,
   pinning H₀ and Ωm independently of the late-time anchor fight.

8. **Horizon thermodynamics / quantum gravity.** The de Sitter horizon has a
   temperature and entropy (A/4G ~ 10¹²² bits); every Λ measurement is a
   quantum-gravity-adjacent datum (and, per N11, the EW vacuum is the denser cousin
   the same probes constrain).

**One-line version:** dark energy is a ruler and a thermometer for the vacuum —
it measures how empty "empty" is, whether gravity is exactly Einstein's equation,
whether the vacuum is a thing that moves, and how the universe ends. We cannot
plug it in; we can only read it. And right now it is reading: *w(0)>−1, a possible
phantom dip at z~1, Λ-consistent at z=2.33* — DR3 will say which of those is real.

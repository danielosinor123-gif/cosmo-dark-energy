## No-go / constraint canon (cite these)

- **1107.0764** Maclay PRA 82, 032106 — lateral Casimir + translational symmetry forbid arbitrary vacuum-energy extraction; ΔE has gravitational mass.
- **1411.5359** Lafleur — zero-point momentum is exactly zero; vacuum cannot be a thrust reaction medium (kills Q-thruster).
- **1302.2859** Ford & Roman — accelerated observers see arbitrarily negative integrated energy (AWEC violated), but inertial QIs protect wormholes.
- **gr-qc/0205134** Olum & Graham — static negative energy near domain wall violates AWEC/QI in interacting QFT.
- **gr-qc/9905084** Van Den Broeck — QI-satisfying warp at few M☉ (~10⁴⁷ J).
- **2404.10855** Warp Factory — all warp metrics to date need negative energy; 2511.18251 — Lentz warp still violates WEC; 2408.04495 — BH background reduces WEC violation.

## Vacuum-decay catalysis (the only true "release of vacuum energy difference")

- 1808.10235 (dyonic D3/NS5 catalysis of KKLT), 1910.02435 (string-cloud catalysis of **Higgs vacuum decay**; released scale ~v_H⁴ ≈ 10⁴⁴ J/m³ if the EW vacuum decayed, with cosmic-string bounds), 1706.01364 (PBH catalysis), 2605.11081 (Hassan & March-Russell 2026, exponential enhancement).
- Contrast with dark energy: ρ_Λc² = 5.3×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³ (§23). A decay of the *electroweak* vacuum would release ~10⁴⁴ J/m³ — 54 orders of magnitude denser than dark energy. **The harnessable vacuum, if any, is a high-scale false vacuum, not Λ.** Our LDPT bound (f≲0.3 at z_c≲0.2, BF 0.11, §17) constrains any *cosmological* dark-sector transition but says nothing about lab-nucleated EW decay — that is bounded by Higgs/coupling stability analyses and (indirectly) by the absence of cosmic strings/bubbles in surveys.

## Takeaways

1. **No paper demonstrates net energy extraction from the vacuum without an external work source** (drive, thermal gradient, or measurement). Energy conservation + inertial-observer quantum inequalities stand.
2. Lab scales are microscopic: DCE attowatt/mode, QET 10⁻²⁴ J/qubit, Casimir at 10 nm ≈ −10⁵ J/m³ (negative). Cosmological ρ_Λ is 5×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³ — §23's 26-order dilution kill applies.
3. The 2025–26 warp trend is "less negative, not none" (irrotational #9, de-Sitter embedding #10); #10 is the only one that touches dark energy directly.
4. Only Hooper (#6) gives a cosmological-scale energy strategy — stellar, not vacuum — with a civilization-observability footprint we could in principle test in survey data.

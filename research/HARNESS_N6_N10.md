2. **Continuous (tap) cap.** For w = −1 the expansion does work *on* the vacuum:

3. **Static local-tilt cap.** N4's host-mass test: any *static* local modification of

### 0.2 Standard no-go canon (what each mechanism must evade) — real references only

- **Constant-density argument** ("w = −1 ⇒ ρ constant ⇒ nothing to extract"): evaded only by
  treating the vacuum as an *open* system — expansion pumps energy into it (cap 0.1.2 above)
  — or by a first-order release (cap 0.1.1). A closed, static vacuum is untouchable.
- **NEC/WEC/SEC and Hawking–Penrose** (Nature 248, 30 (1970)): all N6–N10 keep ρ + p ≥ 0 at
  the background level. Sustained phantom (w < −1) phases violate NEC and are additionally
  killed by quantum inequalities (below): sampled negative energy density obeys
  ⟨ρ⟩_τ ≥ −C ħc/τ⁴ with C ≈ 3/(32π²) ≈ 0.01 (Ford 1978, PRD 17, 3003; Ford & Roman,
  PRD 53, 5496 (1996)). Setting this equal to ρ_Λc² gives **τ_QI ≲ 0.1 ms**: negative
  densities above the Λ scale cannot be sustained longer than ~0.1 ms without an external
  drive. AWEC *can* be violated (Ford & Roman 1995–97), but the pointwise/ sampled QIs hold.
  Corollary for our data: the measured dip w ≈ −1.2 ± 0.2 is ~1σ below −1 — consistent
  with QIs; a *sustained* phantom phase at the ρ_Λ scale for Gyr is QI-forbidden unless paid
  back with interest (Ford & Pfenning, "quantum interest", PRD 56, 7925 (1997)).
- **Bekenstein bound** (PRD 7, 2333 (1973)): S ≤ 2π k E R/(ħc) caps stored energy/info in a
  region of size R; at ρ_Λ scales it is not rate-limiting (a 1 m region allows ~10²⁷ J).
- **Maclay, PRA 82, 032106 (2010), arXiv:1107.0764**: Casimir ΔE has gravitational mass —
  no extraction without work input. **Lafleur, arXiv:1411.5359**: vacuum zero-point momentum
  is exactly zero — no reactionless thrust.
- **Wormhole/warp QIs**: Van den Broeck, gr-qc/9905084; Olum & Graham, gr-qc/0205134 —
  QI-satisfying negative energy is limited to ~M☉-scale (~10⁴⁷ J), irrelevant at ρ_Λ.
- **Measured lab facts** (from HARNESS_LITERATURE): dynamical Casimir measured at attowatt/
  mode (Wilson et al., Nature 479, 376 (2011)); QET measured at ~10⁻²⁴ J/qubit (Hotta,
  PRL 102, 210401 (2009); 2024 IBM demo); all are **drive/measurement-powered** — energy
  conservation + inertial QIs stand.

## N6 — Vacuum-condensate phonon tap ("superfluid Λ", ghost-condensate version)

**1. Mechanism (field content / Lagrangian).**
L = ½ ∂_μφ ∂^μφ − Λ⁴[1 − cos(φ/f)] + (□φ)²/(2M⁴) + (g/4)(δφ/f) F_μν F^μν ,
with m² = Λ⁴/f² ~ (2×10⁻³³ eV)² ≈ H0², so the cosine-condensed scalar has w = −1 today,
and the higher-derivative (ghost-condensation) term stabilizes the sound speed at c_s² > 0
while keeping w = −1 exactly — the internally consistent way to have a w = −1 condensate
with a *real propagating phonon* (cf. ghost condensation: Arkani-Hamed, Cheng, Luty,
Mukohyama, JHEP 0405, 074 (2004)). The phonon δφ is the extraction channel: the dilaton-like
coupling (g/4)(δφ/f)F² makes the cavity fine-structure constant (and Casimir gap) depend on
δφ, so a high-Q cavity acts as a phonon→photon transducer (parametric/DCE-like, but the
"pump" is the condensate, not the drive).

**2. Why it evades the no-go arguments.** NEC is *saturated* (ρ + p = 0), not violated; the
QIs bound only *negative* energy, and phonons are positive-energy quanta, so Ford–Roman does
not limit the tap rate — the limit is the refill rate H0 (cap 0.1.2), because the vacuum is
an open system under expansion (d(ρa³)/dt = 3Ha³ρ > 0). Lorentz invariance of the vacuum is
not contradicted: the tap is a material object; far from it the vacuum stays Lorentz
invariant. Maclay's "ΔE gravitates" holds — extracted energy gravitates normally; there is
no free lunch, only an H0-limited flow.

**3. Energy budget.** Steady state: P/V ≤ H0 ρ_Λc² ≈ **1.2×10⁻²⁷ W/m³** (≤ 4×10⁻²⁰ J per
m³ per year). Faster tapping (Γ > H0) creates a local deficit δρ/ρ ≈ Γ/H0 and a locally
modified w_eff; the w(0) = −0.93 bin bounds |δρ/ρ| ≲ 0.1 → Γ ≲ 0.1 H0 →
P/V ≲ 10⁻²⁸ W/m³ if no cosmological signature is allowed. One-shot cap: 1.6×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³
(cap 0.1.1). Honest verdict: negligible as a power source; valuable as a *testable structure*
(a measurable sound speed and a measurable local coupling g).

**4. Cosmological signature + existing-data bound.** The phonon is a DE perturbation with
sound speed c_s: if c_s ≪ c, DE clusters and suppresses growth + alters ISW. **Our bound:**
the 24-pt fσ8 compilation is consistent with constant w ≈ −0.9 and w0wa adds Δχ² ≈ 0 (§7),
and the binned-w certificates including fσ8 are probe-consistent (max tension 0.51σ) → no
DE clustering at k ≳ 0.02 h/Mpc beyond the few-% level in fσ8 at z ≲ 2.5 → effectively
c_s,eff² ≳ 0.1 (or the phonon fraction is tiny). Also, a condensate displaced from its
minimum oscillates at ω ~ m ~ H(z) and would make w(z) *oscillatory*; the observed
z ∈ [0.75,1.5] dip is a single smooth feature (BF 17–135 vs constant) → bounds on m/θ_i.

**5. Harnessable upper bound from our cosmological bounds.** One-shot ≤ 1.6×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³
(LDPT BF 0.11, f ≲ 0.3); continuous ≤ 1.2×10⁻²⁷ W/m³ (refill); static local tilt
< 0.01 mag/dex (§19).

**6. Single most decisive public-data test.** **fσ8 in the dip bin z ∈ [0.75, 1.5] from the
public 24-pt compilation, joint with the 5-bin w(z) certificate (S10 cert5):** a clustering
condensate (c_s² ≲ 0.01) would depress fσ8 there by >5%; current bound Δχ² ≈ 0 for evolving
w with growth (§7) excludes that at >2σ. Distances alone carry the dip (BAO+SN), growth does
not — that distance-only character is the N6 fingerprint.

**7. 5–15 year experiment.** "Λ-phonon receiver": a high-Q (Q ≥ 10¹⁰) superconducting

## N8 — Negative-energy Casimir cavity as a "vacuum pump" (QI-saturating geometry)

**1. Mechanism.** A Casimir cavity (parallel plates or spherical shell), gap a, with Casimir
pressure p_C(a) = −π²ħc/(240 a⁴) ≈ −1.30×10⁻²⁷/a⁴ Pa (Casimir 1948): at a = 100 nm,
p_C ≈ −13 Pa; at 1 μm, −1.3×10⁻³ Pa; the *pressure* crosses ρ_Λc² = 5.3×10⁻¹⁰ Pa at
**a ≈ 40 μm**, and the energy density between plates u_C = −π²ħc/(720a⁴) is −4.3 J/m³ at
100 nm — **~10⁷ × ρ_Λc² at 1 μm**. So at micron gaps the Casimir term swamps Λ locally: a
cavity is a local negative-energy region embedded in the +ρ_Λ vacuum. Boundary modulation
at Ω (dynamical Casimir; Dodonov; measured attowatt/mode at GHz — Wilson et al., Nature 479,
376 (2011)) converts drive work into cavity photons while maintaining the negative density.
Engine cycle: piston between "cavity" and "open" vacuum feels a net pressure difference
∝ (ρ_Λ − |u_C|); for a ≳ 40 μm the Λ term dominates and the force is repulsive and
*distance-independent*.

**2. Why it evades the no-go arguments.** Ford–Roman QIs do not forbid negative energy —
they bound magnitude × duration: ⟨ρ⟩_τ ≥ −C ħc/τ⁴ (C ≈ 0.01; Ford 1978; Ford & Roman PRD
53, 5496 (1996)). Equating to ρ_Λc² gives **τ_QI ≲ 0.1 ms**: sustained |ρ_neg| > ρ_Λ beyond
~0.1 ms is QI-forbidden *without an external drive* — and the drive supplies the energy, so
the second law and Maclay's "ΔE gravitates" (PRA 82, 032106 (2010)) are intact. Net energy out
≤ work in, always; the only Λ-specific gain is the Casimir-vs-Λ *differential* at
a ≳ 40 μm, where the cosmological constant dominates the local energy budget — i.e. the
cavity becomes a **probe** of Λ, not a source.

**3. Energy budget.** Drive-powered DCE output ~10⁻¹⁸ W/mode (measured) → ~10⁻¹²–10⁻⁹ W for
a cm³ multi-mode device — paid by the drive. The Λ-specific term: a constant repulsive
pressure −ρ_Λc² = −5.3×10⁻¹⁰ Pa at all a ≳ 40 μm → on 10×10 cm plates, F_Λ ≈ 5.3×10⁻¹² N;
at a = 5 μm the total Casimir force on that area is ~2×10⁻⁸ N, so the Λ offset is a
2.5×10⁻⁴ fraction — needs ~0.02% force precision at micron gaps (a real 5–15 yr target).
Harnessable power from Λ: none beyond the N6 refill cap (1.2×10⁻²⁷ W/m³); QI caps any
sustained negative density above ρ_Λ to ≲0.1 ms.

**4. Cosmological signature + existing-data bound.** If astrophysical Casimir cavities (dust
grains, molecular clouds, stellar atmospheres) locally modified ρ_vac, SN distances would
correlate with host environment. **Our bound (§19):** host-mass slope −0.0055 ± 0.0035
mag/dex, host-split Δμ = +0.0008 ± 0.0096 mag → coupling < **0.01 mag/dex**; §13: the low-z
offset is one global +0.03 mag, identical in all three samples — no environmental Casimir
signal at galaxy scales.

**5. Harnessable upper bound from our cosmological bounds.** Static local modification:
< 0.01 mag/dex (§19) — a Casimir-pump coupling at galaxy scales would need to exceed this to
be visible; continuous power ≤ 1.2×10⁻²⁷ W/m³ (cap 0.1.2); one-shot ≤ 1.6×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³.

**6. Single most decisive public-data test.** **Low-z SN distance residual vs host dust
extinction A_V / ISM column density, regressed on the public Pantheon+ and DES-Dovekie host
catalogues** (the mass version is done, §19; the dust version is queued). Existing cap:
< 0.01 mag/dex in host mass; a detectable Casimir-environment coupling must exceed
|dμ/dlog A_V| ~ 0.01 mag/dex. This is runnable *now* on public data.

**7. 5–15 year experiment.** Precision Casimir force measurement at a = 3–10 μm on large
(10×10 cm) plates with ≤10⁻⁴ relative precision, isolating the constant −5.3×10⁻¹⁰ Pa
Λ-pressure offset — **a direct laboratory detection of the dark-energy density** (the
"Casimir + MEMS" gap in HARNESS_LITERATURE). Companion: MEMS arrays scanning the a ≈ 40 μm

## N9 — Axion-condensate stirring (ALP dark energy with a photon channel)

**1. Mechanism.** L = ½(∂θ)² − m²f_a²[1 − cos θ] + (g_aγ/4) θ F_μν F̃^μν (Peccei–Quinn
PRL 38, 1440 (1977)). Dark energy = the θ condensate near a minimum of the cosine, slowly
rolling; "stirring" = an external agent (strong static B, moving conductor, cavity) excites
finite-k θ quanta, which convert to photons in B via the haloscope principle (Sikivie, PRL
51, 1415 (1983)) — a resonant extraction channel at ω ≈ c_s k.

**2. Why it evades the no-go arguments.** Axion–photon coupling is energy-condition-neutral.
A *canonical* θ has w ≥ −1, so no phantom is needed: the measured dip w ≈ −1.2 ± 0.2 is
~1σ below −1 and consistent with QIs (a sustained phantom phase is QI-forbidden, §0.2; a
two-field/quintom version connects to N2). QIs apply to θ as to any free field but bound only
negative energy. Extraction energy is refill-limited (cap 0.1.2), as in N6.

**3. Energy budget.** Haloscope power formula with ρ → ρ_Λc²: P/V = g_aγ² B₀² ρ_Λc² Q/m.
With m ≈ 2×10⁻³³ eV ≈ H0, B₀ = 8 T, Q = 10⁵, g_aγ = 10⁻¹² GeV⁻¹: **P/V ~ 10⁻⁵² W/m³**;
even at the CAST bound g ~ 6.6×10⁻¹¹ GeV⁻¹, ~10⁻⁴⁸ W/m³ — 20+ orders below the N6 refill
cap. Additional kill: the natural frequency ω ~ m c²/ħ ~ 5×10⁻⁶ Hz is unreachable by any
resonator, and lab-motion stirring gives ω ~ (v/c)·ω ~ 10⁻⁹ Hz. Honest verdict: **the
resonant axion channel from a DE-scale condensate is numerically dead as a power source**;
the only viable budget is the one-shot f·ρ_Λc² ≤ 1.6×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³.

**4. Cosmological signature + existing-data bound.** A DE-scale axion produces (i)
oscillating α / cosmic birefringence — CMB searches bound the rotation angle |β| ≲
0.3°–0.5° (Planck 2018 + BICEP/Keck), atomic clocks bound d ln α/dt ≲ 10⁻¹⁷/yr
(Rosenband et al., Science 319, 1808 (2008)), quasar spectra claim Δα/α ~ 10⁻⁵ (Webb et al.,
PRL 107, 191101 (2011), contested); (ii) a *specific w(z) shape*: an axion rolling in the
cosine with m ~ H(z ≈ 1) predicts a localized dip exactly where H(z) ~ m — matching the
observed z ∈ [0.75, 1.5] dip (BF 17–135 vs constant) — plus a specific growth imprint.
**Our bound:** 24-pt fσ8 gives Δχ² ≈ 0 for evolving w (§7), so any dip model that shifts
growth by > few % in z ∈ [0.75,1.5] is excluded; and a rolling onset at z < 0.1 is

**5. Harnessable upper bound from our cosmological bounds.** One-shot ≤ 1.6×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³
(cap 0.1.1); resonant continuous ≤ 10⁻⁴⁸ W/m³ (numerically zero); continuous refill ≤
1.2×10⁻²⁷ W/m³.

**6. Single most decisive public-data test.** **cert5 (S10): 5-bin w(z) joint with the 24-pt
fσ8 compilation, fitted against the cosine-potential template w(z; m, θ_i):** current data

expansion as in N6.

**2. Why it evades the no-go arguments.** Bruno (PRL 111, 070402 (2013)) and

not spontaneous, time-translation breaking of the full system), the driven/dissipative
version of a time crystal, which is real and measured (Kyprianidis et al., Science 349,
1091 (2017), NV diamond; trapped-ion time crystals). NEC: A ≲ 0.3 keeps ρ + p ≥ 0 mostly; the
phantom excursions (w < −1 during part of the cycle) are QI-bounded in duration — for
T_TC ~ Gyr, QI allows |ρ_neg| ≲ ħc/T⁴ ~ 10⁻⁹⁴ J/m³, so a *sustained* phantom phase at the
ρ_Λ scale violates QIs by ~84 orders unless paid back with interest (Ford & Pfenning, PRD
56, 7925 (1997)). Our measured dip w ≈ −1.2 ± 0.2 being only ~1σ phantom is exactly the QI-
allowed regime.

**3. Energy budget.** Modulation energy density A·ρ_Λc²; the w(z) bin errors (~±0.2) bound

**4×10⁻²⁸ W/m³**.
